Hook: Card game where you race to empty your hand by matching cards,
except cards go face down so you can lie, bluff, and call out your friends
Overview: Digital card game for 3 players on Miro, it mixes simple color
and number matching and throws bluffing into the mix. The goal is to reach
zero cards or outlast the other players. Any card can be played face down
to trick others. Power cards also bring more chaos by letting players punish
others, or get punished by being called out.
Gameplay: Digital card game for 3 players using color and number
matching cards mixed with power cards. The goal is to either get your hand
to zero or survive the other players getting too many and losing. Players
are able to play power cards that can offer benefits and can punish other
players. Any card can be placed face down to attempt to trick other players,
and calling out a player for not playing what they say will punish them
depending on the card, and if the person who made the call is wrong, they
will have to draw a reduced number of cards. Cards numbered 1-3 draw 2,
4-6 Draw 4, and 7-8 Draw 6. Power cards called out will allow the person
who correctly got it to use the power as if it were their own card. Players
start with a hand of 6 randomly drawn cards. No Point system, just getting
cards to zero. Any player with over 15 cards will lose and the game will
continue until the last player stands. The deck will be shuffled when
needed.
Style: The game will use a simple design style similar to uno to avoid cards
looking too complex, but not too boring either. Each card will be given a
color and a number, and power cards will have their own designs following
the same idea.
MVP: The MVP will be one complete deck on Miro with the rulebook.
32 Number Cards with 4 colors numbered 1-8, with the penalty being cards
numbered 1-3 draw 2, 4-6 Draw 4, and 7-8 Draw 6
16 Power Cards, 4 Card Swap, 4 Reverse, 4 Skip, and 4 Hand Swaps
Face Down Covers for each card
Cards will be designed and built in Miro, with Separate Face down versions
One Page Rulebook
Board Layout with card areas
Planned Blind playtest from a separate group of people to test the flow
